## About

HUMSAFAR, a support centre in crisis, <cite index="7-7">was set up in Lucknow in November 2003 to ensure a holistic response to women's gender and human rights violations and to work towards gender equality</cite>. <cite index="11-1">It was formally registered in 2008 to ensure a holistic feminist response to discrimination and violence across the sexual spectrum based on gender, religion, caste, class, and ethnicity.</cite> <cite index="7-7">It has been collectively managed by a group of Trustees, full-time trained and experienced staff, and a large number of vigilant volunteers working across communities, educational establishments, and among professionals.</cite>

The organization takes a multi-pronged approach to addressing gender-based violence. Its casework unit — the curative arm of the organization — provides a wide range of support services to women survivors, including paralegal aid, legal assistance, medical support, social mediation, counseling, rescue operations, shelter, and rehabilitation. Over the past decade-plus, HUMSAFAR has intervened in thousands of cases (figures cited across sources range from roughly 7,000 to over 10,000).

## Scope of Work

<cite index="7-1">HUMSAFAR currently works in 53 neighborhoods with women and in 42 schools and colleges with youth across the Lucknow division, spanning the districts of Lucknow, Hardoi, Unnao, and Sitapur.</cite> <cite index="7-1,7-2">It also partners with the Uttar Pradesh Department of Women and Child Welfare to train Anganwadi workers under the Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) program on gender rights and violence. Believing that civil society must be engaged to achieve zero violence, HUMSAFAR works with a broad network of professionals — lawyers, doctors, businesspeople, homemakers, teachers, school principals, and retired judges — known collectively as "Friends of HUMSAFAR."</cite>

<cite index="7-2,7-3">A related youth initiative, "Yuva Tarang," is made up of young people drawn from neighborhood committees, schools, and colleges. Through its casework, HUMSAFAR has found that women often delay leaving abusive relationships because they feel ill-equipped to rebuild their lives independently — a finding that shapes its focus on holistic rehabilitation, not just crisis intervention.</cite>

## Services Offered

- **Paralegal & legal support** – assistance navigating legal processes for survivors
- **Medical support** – referrals and assistance accessing medical care
- **Social mediation & counseling** – psychosocial support for individuals and families
- **Rescue, shelter & rehabilitation** – crisis intervention and longer-term recovery support
- **Community & school programs** – awareness and prevention work in neighborhoods, schools, and colleges
- **Training programs** – e.g., POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) training for youth leaders in organized and unorganized sectors; gender-rights training for Anganwadi workers

## Notes on Data Discrepancies

- Case-intervention figures vary slightly by source and publication date: older listings cite ~7,000 cases over ten years, while the organization's own LinkedIn "About" text cites more than 10,000 cases over ten years — likely reflecting different points in time as the count has grown.
- Employee count is listed as "2–10" on LinkedIn and "~6" on RocketReach, consistent with a small, staff-plus-volunteer nonprofit structure.
- The organization is sometimes referred to as "HUMSAFAR, a Support Centre for Women in Crisis" (earlier framing) versus "Support Centre for Women, Youth and Queer Persons" (current, broader framing) — reflecting an expansion of mandate over time toward youth and LGBTQIA+ inclusion.
- Note: HUMSAFAR India (Lucknow) is a distinct organization from "Humsafar Trust" (humsafar.org), a Mumbai-based LGBTQ+ health and support organization — the two are unrelated despite similar names.
